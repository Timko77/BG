Our story

We started with used cars. Today we take care of everything your car needs.

For more than 25 years, B&G Avtomobili d.o.o. has been keeping drivers happy on the road. We started out buying and selling used cars; today we also import cars to order and offer everything you need to buy, finance and maintain a car under one roof: servicing, bodywork and paintwork, tyre services, car cleaning and car insurance.

We're not a big chain. We're a team of more than twenty people from Šentjur and the surrounding area who greet you by name, tell you what needs doing and what doesn't – and keep our word. That's why customers come back to us for their second, third and fourth car.
